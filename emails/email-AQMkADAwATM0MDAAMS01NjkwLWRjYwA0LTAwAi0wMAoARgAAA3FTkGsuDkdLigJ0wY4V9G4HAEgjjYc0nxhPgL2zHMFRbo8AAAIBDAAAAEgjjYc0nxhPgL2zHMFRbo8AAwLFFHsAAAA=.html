When it happens inside the world’s most powerful democracy, it happens to all of us. It’s time for all of us to say NO, Akein. 

Last week, I shared with you Mahmoud Khalil’s story of being targeted and punished simply for peacefully speaking out against genocide.  

Mahmoud’s detention is not an isolated case. Across the USA, activists and academics face the same tactics: detention without charge, law weaponised as punishment, speech silenced through fear. 

Authoritarianism doesn’t stay contained - it creeps into countries and institutions that once seemed safe.  

DONATE NOW TO REJECT AUTHORITARIANISM

In the USA, civic space is narrowing: journalists intimidated, protesters punished, universities and civil society silenced, and communities dehumanised. Rule-of-law safeguards are weakening: due process bypassed, courts and lawyers intimidated and oversight dismantled. 

There is a playbook. A set of tactics used by repressive governments to silence dissent, crush opposition, and tighten their grip on power. We've seen it in Afghanistan, China and Myanmar. Now we're seeing it in the USA. 

This is what the playbook looks like when its full force is directed: 

  • Identify the voice that challenges you  

  • Claim a legal pretext - however thin  

  • Move fast, before lawyers or courts can intervene  

  • Stir up fear and hatred against your victim  

  • Make them an example to deter others  

  • Let fear do the rest 

The Trump administration did not invent the authoritarian playbook. It has accelerated it.  

DONATE TO FUND THE COUNTER-PLAYBOOK

Amnesty has fought the authoritarian playbook before. And won:

  • The International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Taliban leaders for crimes against women and girls.

  • Rodrigo Duterte, who oversaw the extrajudicial killing of thousands in the Philippines, was arrested and transferred to the ICC.

  • After 104 days in ICE detention, Mahmoud Khalil was released following sustained international pressure - including thousands of Australians who signed Amnesty’s petition. 

This is the counter-playbook: document the abuse, build international pressure, pursue accountability. It works when it is funded and when it is deployed early. 

Your support directly funds Amnesty’s research capacity, legal advocacy and rapid-response campaigning - the tools that turn documentation into pressure, and pressure into change. 

We don’t just say NO. We fight back ✊
